Plan Your Trip

Route: Lahore to Hunza

  1. Lahore to Islamabad by motorway (4 hours)
  2. Islamabad to Besham via Karakoram Highway
  3. Besham to Chilas, overnight stay
  4. Chilas to Gilgit, see the Rakaposhi viewpoint
  5. Gilgit to Karimabad, Hunza Valley

Packing Checklist

  • Warm jacket and thermal layers
  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Power bank and camera
  • Original CNIC or passport
